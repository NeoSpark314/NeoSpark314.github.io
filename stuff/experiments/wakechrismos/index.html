📜 Grimoire of Awakening: Crucial Details

1. Why web browsers can't send raw UDP WoL packets:
Web browsers are strictly sandboxed and cannot transmit raw Layer 2 UDP broadcast packets (port 9) over a local network or WAN.

2. Why wake already works for Minecraft:
Your server is reported as Powered by AMP (CubeCoders AMP). AMP has an Application Sleep feature: when players disconnect, the heavy server hibernates and AMP listens on port 25565. As soon as a client pings or connects, AMP awakens the game! Clicking "Awaken Realm" sends immediate Minecraft Server List Ping (SLP) probes to trigger this.

3. If the host PC is completely powered off (ACPI S5):
If the entire physical computer is shut down, standard routers drop broadcast packets from the Internet. You will need either: • A port forward on your router directing UDP port 9 to your broadcast IP (or ARP binding), or
• A tiny WoL bridge script (or Home Assistant/router) inside the LAN.
